Hi all,

The NATRAX spend and programme updates you have been getting by mail are now
kept live in a web app: **https://sightlinevalidation.web.app**

It holds one record for the SightLine PoCs at NATRAX — every track session, the
invoices raised against them, PO drawdown, manpower days and workshop rental.
The numbers on screen are the same ones the monthly report is built from, so
there is no second version to reconcile.

**What you can see**

| | |
|---|---|
| Projects | Spend and sessions per programme — Mahindra EV, Mahindra ICE, Tata Harrier EV, Kia Sonet |
| Analyser | Cost by month, by track and by vehicle |
| PO Tracker | What each PO has left, against the invoices actually raised |
| Muster | Manpower days booked and who was on site |
| Updates | Programme status and what changed since the last look |

**To get on the monthly report**

The monthly PoC cost report goes out by email. To add yourself or someone else:

1. Open the app and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → Communication Hub → Email Reports**.
3. Tap **Add Subscriber** and enter the name and email address.

Anyone on that list is copied on every monthly report from then on. Taking
someone off is the same screen.

**To get an account**

Open the link and choose **Create Profile** on the sign-in card. Please use your
Goodyear address. Accounts are read-only by default — you will see everything
and change nothing, which is what most of you want. If you need to enter
sessions or invoices, tell me and I will switch it on for your account.

Happy to walk anyone through it on a call.

Regards,
Dhrupad
