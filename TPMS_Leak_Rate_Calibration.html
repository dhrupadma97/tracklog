1. System Definition

The TPMS ECU stores a Leak Rate → Interval lookup table. For each Leak Rate category the ECU monitors pressure over the corresponding Interval window. If the observed pressure drop within that window exceeds an internal threshold, a leak alert is triggered.

ΔPthreshold = Leak Rate × Interval The product of Leak Rate × Interval defines the pressure-drop detection threshold for each setpoint
ECU Leak Rate (system units)Nature of LeakInterval direction
30,000 — 20,000Fast (large orifice)Short window
15,000 — 5,000MediumModerate window
3,500 — 1,000Slow (small orifice / diffusion)Long window

2. Governing Physics

For a leak through a small orifice (choked or near-choked subsonic flow), the mass outflow rate is proportional to the upstream (tyre) absolute pressure. Applying ideal-gas conservation inside a fixed-volume tyre:

dP/dt ∝ Pabs / Vair Pabs = absolute tyre pressure (gauge + 101.3 kPa)  |  Vair = internal air volume of tyre

The time required to observe a fixed pressure drop ΔP therefore scales inversely — a tyre with a larger volume or lower inflation pressure exhibits a slower dP/dt and needs a longer detection window. Because both volume and pressure are captured simultaneously during a physical measurement, the calibration method below does not require either to be known or calculated separately.

3. Calibration Method — Empirical 60-Second Test

A 1.5 mm standard reference orifice is used as the calibration stimulus. By measuring the actual pressure drop over 60 seconds on the mounted tyre, the system's Leak Rate unit is derived directly from hardware — no tyre geometry or volume estimation needed.

3.1 Measurement

Inflate tyre to manufacturer's recommended cold pressure. Insert 1.5 mm orifice. After exactly 60 seconds, record:

Ploss = P₀ − P₁    (kPa) P₀ = initial pressure  |  P₁ = pressure at 60 s

3.2 Discovered Leak Rate

Normalise the 1-minute measurement to the ECU's internal daily-rate scale (1440 = minutes per day):

LRdiscovered = Ploss × 1440 Units: kPa · min⁻¹ × min·day⁻¹ → ECU daily-rate scale

3.3 Scale Multiplier (per setpoint)

For each desired Leak Rate setpoint in the ECU table, compute an individual multiplier:

M = LRdiscovered ÷ LRdesired Calculate M separately for each of the 8 setpoints

3.4 Calibrated Interval

Icalibrated = Ibase × M Ibase = platform base interval from system specification  |  Round to nearest 5 ms per ECU timer resolution
Why this works for any tyre Because P_loss is measured on the actual mounted tyre at its actual inflation pressure, the effects of tyre volume, construction, temperature, and pressure are all captured implicitly. No tyre size, volume, or pressure input is required in the formulas above.
